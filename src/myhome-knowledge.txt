ABOUT MY HOME CONSTRUCTIONS
My Home Constructions is one of Hyderabad's leading real estate developers with over 35 years of experience. We build premium residential apartments, gated communities, and integrated townships across Hyderabad. We are known for quality construction, timely delivery, and large-scale amenities. Our RERA registrations are available for all active projects.

ONGOING VENTURES OVERVIEW
We currently have multiple residential ventures across Hyderabad in Kokapet, Tellapur, Kollur, and Gachibowli. Each venture offers different configurations, price points, and possession timelines. Our flagship luxury projects are in the Financial District and Kokapet zones.

MY HOME APAS - KOKAPET
My Home Apas is a premium high-rise project located in Kokapet, near the Outer Ring Road and Financial District. It offers 3BHK and 4BHK luxury apartments. The 3BHK units range from 2,400 to 2,800 square feet, priced between 2.8 crore and 3.4 crore rupees. The 4BHK units range from 3,600 to 4,200 square feet, priced between 4.5 crore and 5.5 crore rupees. The project has 5 towers, each 40 floors high. Possession is expected by December 2026. Amenities include a 50,000 square foot clubhouse, swimming pools, tennis courts, and landscaped gardens. The price per square foot is approximately 12,000 rupees.

MY HOME AKARA - KOKAPET
My Home Akara is located in Kokapet, close to My Home Apas. It offers spacious 3BHK and 4BHK apartments aimed at premium buyers. The 3BHK units are around 2,600 square feet starting at 3 crore rupees. The 4BHK units are around 4,000 square feet starting at 4.8 crore rupees. Possession is expected by mid 2027. The project features a grand clubhouse, indoor games, a mini theatre, and a senior citizen zone.

MY HOME TARKSHYA - KOKAPET
My Home Tarkshya is an ultra-luxury project in Kokapet offering only 4BHK apartments. The units range from 4,500 to 5,200 square feet, priced from 6 crore rupees upward. The price per square foot is approximately 13,500 rupees. Possession is expected by 2028. This project targets high-net-worth buyers and features private lifts, sky lounges, and concierge services.

MY HOME 99 - TELLAPUR
My Home 99 is located in Tellapur, a fast-growing residential corridor. It offers 2BHK, 3BHK, and 4BHK apartments at more accessible price points. MY HOME VIHANGA - GACHIBOWLI
My Home Vihanga is a completed and fully occupied project in Gachibowli. It offers 2BHK and 3BHK apartments. Resale units are occasionally available. The 2BHK units are around 1,300 square feet and the 3BHK units are around 1,800 square feet. Since this is a ready-to-move completed project, prices depend on resale market rates, typically 9,500 to 10,500 rupees per square foot. This project is popular due to its proximity to IT offices.

MY HOME BHOOJA - KOLLUR
My Home Bhooja is a large gated community in Kollur, near the Outer Ring Road. It offers 3BHK apartments designed for families. The units range from 1,700 to 2,100 square feet, priced between 1.3 crore and 1.7 crore rupees. The price per square foot is approximately 7,500 rupees. Possession is expected by March 2027. It is one of our most affordable premium options with strong amenities including a clubhouse, sports facilities, and large open spaces.

BOOKING PROCESS
To book an apartment, customers pay a booking amount of 5 lakh rupees to block a unit. Within 15 days, an agreement of sale is signed and 20 percent of the total cost must be paid. The remaining amount is paid in construction-linked installments. We assist with home loans from major banks including HDFC, SBI, ICICI, and Axis Bank. Most leading banks have pre-approved our projects.

HOME LOAN ASSISTANCE
We have tie-ups with all major banks for home loans. Loans are available up to 80 percent of the property value. Our sales team helps customers with loan documentation and approvals. Interest rates depend on the bank and the customer's credit profile.

SITE VISITS
We offer free site visits for all our ventures. Customers can book a site visit by sharing their name, phone number, preferred venture, and preferred date. We provide cab pickup and drop for site visits to Kokapet and Kollur projects. Site visits are available all seven days from 10 AM to 6 PM.

AMENITIES GENERAL
All our premium projects include large clubhouses, swimming pools, gymnasiums, indoor games, children's play areas, jogging tracks, landscaped gardens, 24x7 security, power backup, and ample parking. Luxury projects in Kokapet additionally offer concierge services, sky lounges, and private theatres.

POSSESSION AND DELAYS
Possession timelines are shared at the time of booking and mentioned in the agreement. In case of construction delays, customers are compensated as per RERA guidelines. We have a strong track record of on-time or early delivery.

CONTACT AND SUPPORT
Our sales offices are located at each project site and at our corporate office in Banjara Hills. Sales support is available from 9 AM to 8 PM. For booking, pricing, or site visit queries, our agents can assist. For complex legal or financial queries, customers can be connected to a senior relationship manager.
